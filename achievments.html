Quadcopter Challenge

I also took part in the quadcopter challenge. In this challenge, I was part of a team of 5 students, and we all had to work together to design and build our own quadcopter! First, we had a qualification round where we had to create and display our idea for a quadcopter on the theme of “local heroes”. We decided to choose the theme of search and rescue and chose the name “Search 4 Life”. We then had to make a PowerPoint about our ideas and send it to the judges, luckily, we made it into the next round! Next was the Regionals. This is when we got to build and fly our quadcopter. We first had a build day, where we were given the laser cut template of the basic structure of our quadcopter and the electronic components and we had to assemble and test it. After that we had time for modifications, this was a key point in the process that got us into the next round. Modifications were things such as, new colours, legs, cameras etc. Anything that would help make our drone more useful and adapted to the role we had chosen for it. Some of the modifications that we had included were, longer arms for our drone (as a safety precaution in case it crashed), longer legs with flat circular ends (to make the landing sturdier and more cushioned and to protect the battery during landing) and an extra set of roofs (because they protect the exposed electronic components and we broke all the ones we were given). We also had to choose our roles amongst us in the team. I was first positioned as the head pilot and got my own fliers ID but was then moved to head of modifications and co-pilot. During the Finals of the Regionals, we came first as our drone model, presentation and piloting exceeded the other teams. Since making it through the Regionals, we then went to London for the Nationals. This is where the winner of the Quadcopter challenge would be decided. There were 13 other schools and Kadet groups. In the end, we unfortunately didn’t place in any of the competitions but we did make it into the top 14 out of the 400 other schools competing from around the country and even Scottland!
